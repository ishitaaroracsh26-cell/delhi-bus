1. Executive Summary & Problem Resolution

DilliTransit is a comprehensive public transit journey planner and probe-vehicle traffic velocity estimator engineered specifically for the National Capital Territory (NCT) of Delhi and the National Capital Region (NCR). It resolves systemic commuter pain points and critical gaps found in legacy transit apps (such as One Delhi, Chartr, and Google Maps) by combining DTC and DIMTS cluster buses, DMRC Metro Lines (Yellow, Blue, Pink, Airport Express), RapidX (NCRTC RRTS), and last-mile feeder networks (E-rickshaws, shared autos) into a single, seamless, interactive platform.

🗺️ Google Maps-Styled Interface

Default high-definition Google Maps Road raster tile integration, animated live buses, traffic speed segments, and 5-way satellite/hybrid/dark tile switcher.

💺 Movie-Theater Seat Selection

Interactive 37-seat bus layout preventing boarding time chaos. Issues digital anti-chaos boarding passes with PNR tokens and anti-crowd guarantees.

🌸 Saheli Pink Women Buses

Dedicated female special bus section (Seats W1–W8) and automatic ₹0 Free Pink Pass verification under the Delhi Government scheme.

⚡ Rush Bypass Alternate Routes

Automatic detection of corridor bottlenecks ($CF > 1.8$, speed < 16 km/h) with 1-click bypass redirection via underground Metro or RapidX.

🚔 Delhi Police & Safety Layer

10 mapped Delhi Police stations with hotline dials (112 / 1091), well-lit night safety ratings (4.8+★), and 1-click live GPS WhatsApp SOS dispatch.

📍 Hyper-Local Stop Intelligence

Exact roadside boarding bay pins (e.g. Bay 2 opposite OPD Gate), specific Metro exit gates for feeder autos, and prominent local landmarks.

💳 Offline-First NCMC Calculator

Calculates exact 10% RuPay One Delhi / NCMC card discounts and projected monthly commuter savings (discount × 44 trips).

⚡ Zero-Dependency Stack

Pure Python 3.14 standard library backend with multi-threaded HTTP server. Zero external pip packages, starts in under 50ms, 30/30 tests passing.

2. Mathematical Traffic & Physics Formulation

Traditional navigation apps falsely misidentify bus stops as severe traffic bottlenecks because buses pause to board passengers. DilliTransit models passenger dwell friction separately from roadway link velocities using probe-vehicle velocity estimation (PVVE):

Mathematical Model Equation Parameters & Interpretation
1. Probe Velocity Derivation $$ar{v}_{ ext{segment}} = rac{\sum_{i=1}^N \Delta d_i}{\sum_{i=1}^N \Delta t_i}$$ $\Delta d_i$: Haversine distance between consecutive GPS pings; $\Delta t_i$: Elapsed telemetry interval.
2. Dwell Time Separation $$t_{ ext{dwell}}(s) = au_0 + lpha_{ ext{hub}} \cdot \mathbb{I}_{ ext{hub}}(s) + eta_{ ext{peak}}$$ $ au_0 = 25 ext{s}$ baseline boarding;
$lpha_{ ext{hub}} = 25 ext{s}$ terminal hub friction;
$eta_{ ext{peak}} = 10 ext{s}$ peak rush friction.
3. Congestion Factor ($CF$) $$CF = rac{V_{ ext{free-flow}}}{V_{ ext{observed}}}$$ $CF > 1.8$: Severe Bottleneck;
$CF \in [1.3, 1.8]$: Moderate Delay;
$CF < 1.3$: Free Flow.
4. Dynamic Multimodal ETA $$ ext{ETA} = \sum_{k} rac{d_k}{V_k(t)} + \sum_{s} t_{ ext{dwell}}(s) + \sum_{m} (t_{ ext{walk}} + t_{ ext{wait}})$$ Integrates road speed, stop dwell times, and platform interchange walking buffers.

3. Movie-Style Bus Seat Selection Architecture

To solve Delhi's pervasive boarding chaos, DilliTransit introduces an airline/movie-theater seat reservation system for buses. Before boarding, commuters view an interactive 37-seat layout:

Pink Zone (W1–W8): Women Priority (₹0 Pink Pass eligible)
Accessible (S1–S4): Senior & Disabled Priority
General (G1–G20): Standard 2x2 Seating
Lounge (R1–R5): Rear Bench

Upon locking a seat, the system issues a tamper-evident digital boarding pass with an official PNR (e.g. DT26-54278), Anti-Chaos Token (e.g. DEL-AC-912), and seat badge. Showing this pass guarantees seat allocation and eliminates door pushing.
