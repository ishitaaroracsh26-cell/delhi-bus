Delhi Bus Traffic Model (PVVE)

Probe-Vehicle Velocity Estimation: Operating buses serve as moving sensors across Delhi arterials. Instantaneous velocity is derived from consecutive GPS pings:

v_probe = Δdistance / Δtime

Separation of Dwell & Delay: Passenger boarding time at bus stops is isolated so that passenger stops aren't falsely reported as traffic bottlenecks:

t_dwell = τ_base (25s) + α_hub (25s)

Congestion Factor (CF): Ratio of arterial free-flow speed to measured corridor bus speed:

CF = V_freeflow / V_observed  (Green: <1.2, Red: >1.8)

Data Attribution & Official OTD Setup

1. Current Data Status

This deployment is currently operating in Sample & Calibrated Physics Mode. All route lines, stop locations, frequencies, and fares reflect genuine Delhi Transport Corporation (DTC) and DIMTS cluster bus schedules. Real-time GPS pings are computed using the calibrated Probe-Vehicle Velocity Estimator (PVVE).

2. How to Connect Official Live Delhi Transit Data

To connect live vehicle positions directly from the Govt. of NCT of Delhi transport department:

  1. Register an account on the Delhi Open Transit Data portal under the Real Time Data section.
  2. Acquire your private secret API key.
  3. Add the key to your environment or .env file:
DELHI_OTD_API_KEY="your_secret_otd_api_key_here"

The backend will automatically detect the key, authenticate against https://otd.delhi.gov.in/api/realtime/VehiclePositions.pb, and upgrade the interface to LIVE OTD GTFS FEED.

3. Separation of Measured Data vs. Assumptions

  • Measured Data: Bus vehicle IDs (e.g. DL-1PD-4281), GPS latitude/longitude, instantaneous speeds, and route sequences.
  • Assumptions & Physics Model: Off-peak free-flow corridor baselines (32–55 km/h), nominal dwell times (25s base + 25s for major interchange hubs), and interchange walking speeds (4.5 km/h).
